# Mental-Health-Analysis

Which workplace factors predict whether a tech employee seeks treatment for a mental health condition? A Boston University MS in Business Analytics team project by Bhagirath Bhardwaj, Vibhas Goel, Lakshmi Sindhura Uppalapati and Valentina Torres.

- **Data:** the Open Sourcing Mental Health survey of people working in tech.
- **Method:** cleaning and exploratory analysis, feature selection, then logistic regression, a support vector classifier, random forest, XGBoost and a voting classifier, tuned with grid and randomized search.
- **Evaluation:** confusion matrix, a custom cost function, balanced accuracy and ROC curves, plus feature importances.

Open `mental_health_analysis.ipynb`, or use the Colab badge at the top of the notebook.
